Purpose and authorized use

Atlas Connector is a private Windows utility for internal administration of Sweet Deals PDX LLC's QuickBooks Online accounting data. It is not marketed, distributed, or offered as a public subscription or consumer service. Use is limited to individuals authorized by Sweet Deals PDX LLC and by the relevant QuickBooks account owner or administrator.

Scope of the current utility

Phase 1 provides Intuit OAuth authorization, token refresh, company verification, an account lookup associated with “7354,” and retrieval and local searching of general-ledger transaction rows. The current utility does not create, edit, delete, import, reconcile, or otherwise write accounting transactions. Additional capabilities, if developed, will be subject to separate review, authorization, and appropriate controls.

Intuit and third-party services

Use of QuickBooks Online and Intuit's services is subject to applicable Intuit agreements, access permissions, and availability. The operator must have valid rights to the connected company data. Atlas Connector is independently developed for internal use; it is not an endorsement by Intuit.
